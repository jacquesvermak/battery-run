How to Play

  • Swipe left/right, tap ◀ ▶, or use the arrow keys to move between 3 lanes.
  • Collect ☀️ Solar, 🔋 Batteries and 💰 Coins for points — solar and batteries also recharge your battery.
  • Avoid ⚡ Electrical hazards, ☁️ Clouds and 💥 Energy monsters — they drain your battery.
  • Your battery drains a little on its own too, even without a hit — keep collecting to stay charged.
  • Speed steps up every 10 seconds, and again every time you pass 500m, 1,000m or 2,000m.
  • You've got 90 seconds. The run ends the moment either the clock or your battery hits zero — see how far you can get!
